Reinforcement Learning for Elegant Walking in Quadruped Robots

Trung Dang*, Anh Tran*
University of Massachusetts Amherst
IEEE Robotics and Automation Conference 2024
*Equal Contribution
Paper Supplementary Code arXiv

Abstract

Recent advancements in locomotion control have significantly improved the agility and stability of robots. Among these developments, Reinforcement Learning (RL) has emerged as a promising approach, enabling robots—particularly quadruped robots—to tackle remarkably challenging tasks, such as parkour and navigating complex, unstructured terrains. In this project, we propose a training pipeline to achieve stable and refined walking patterns in quadruped robots, characterized by natural, symmetric gaits. Additionally, we explore using a depth camera to enhance the robot's ability to climb stairs and navigate rough terrains.
